Compound writes LinkedIn posts for technical B2B companies that have real expertise and a weak LinkedIn presence.

B2B buyers evaluate you on LinkedIn before they call. Compound makes sure you've got something worth reading.

Who this is for

For B2B companies that haven't figured out what to say on LinkedIn — or are saying it without a system.

Compound takes what a technical B2B company actually does, works out what's worth saying, and turns it into LinkedIn posts that explain it clearly. The company publishes them under its own name.

The Model

How it actually runs.

01

Diagnostic

One week. A clear read on what the company is saying today, what it should be saying instead, and where to start.

02

Understand

Conversations, plus whatever's already on hand — old decks, website copy, anything useful.

03

Find the story

What the company should be saying on LinkedIn, specifically. Not a rebrand, not new positioning — just the right thing, said the right way, in this one place.

04

Build the system

A repeatable voice, a set of themes, and a simple way to find the next post topic.

What Compound Delivers

Start with the diagnostic.

One week, one clear answer: a short written memo — a few pages, not a deck — on what's actually wrong with how the company shows up today.

From there, BUILD runs four weeks and ends with eight LinkedIn posts, written and ready to publish, plus a system for writing the next ones.

Eight posts, written from the same system, published under your name. That's what going from invisible to present actually looks like.

RUN is optional — Compound keeps writing on an ongoing basis. The client always owns their own account and their own inbox.

Pricing is based on what the diagnostic finds. You'll know the number before BUILD starts.

Early Work

An AI company building agents for financial-services workflows shared a sales deck they wanted to sharpen. I ran it through a structured process of research, analysis, and independent verification.

A creative production studio serving prestige beauty brands had a story that could have belonged to almost any studio in the category.

FAQ

What do I get for the diagnostic?

A short written memo on what the company is saying today, what it should be saying, and the one gap worth fixing first. No commitment beyond that.

Why only LinkedIn, and only eight posts?

Because that's where the people evaluating a B2B company actually look first. Eight posts is enough to go from invisible to present — to have something coherent in market. Whether it's working becomes clear over the following weeks, which is what RUN is for.

Who is this for?

B2B companies with real expertise and a weak LinkedIn presence — technical, specialist businesses that haven't figured out what to say, or are saying it without a system.

Do you manage my LinkedIn account?

No. I write the posts. You publish them and own your own inbox.
